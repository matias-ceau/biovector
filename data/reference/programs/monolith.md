# Building the Monolith

> Wendler's 6-week high-volume program — 3×/week, percentage-based

**Status:** Template only (never logged in sets.json)  
**Source:** Jim Wendler's "Building the Monolith" program

---

## Structure

6-week cycle, 3 workouts per week (A/B/C). Percentages of training max.

### Workout A — Squat + Press

| Exercise | Wk 1 | Wk 2 | Wk 3 | Wk 4 | Wk 5 | Wk 6 |
|----------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| Squat (S00) | 70,80,90,90,90,90,90 | 65,75,85,85,85,85,85 | 75,85,95,95,95,95,95 | 70,80,90,90,90,90,90 | 65,75,85,85,85,85,85 | 75,85,95,95,95,95,95 |
| Military Press (P10.0) | 70,80,90,70 | 65,75,85,65 | 75,85,95,75 | 70,80,90,70 | 65,75,85,65 | 75,85,95,75 |

Last MP set = AMRAP

Accessories:
- Chin-ups (T00): 100 reps total
- Band Face Pulls (T30.1): 100 reps total
- Dips (P20): 100 reps total

---

## Progression

After completing the 6-week block:
- Increase training max by 2.5 kg (upper) / 5 kg (lower)
- Repeat the 6-week cycle

This program was defined as a template in the codebase but was never actually run. The percentage arrays correspond to weeks 1–6, with each value being a percentage of the training max for that exercise.
