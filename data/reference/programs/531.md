# 5/3/1

> Wendler's 5/3/1 — percentage-based periodization over 4-week cycles

**Used:** 2018-07 to 2022-04 (various configurations)  
**Total logged:** ~2820 sets across ~147 sessions

---

## Core percentages

4-week cycle, percentages of training max (TM ≈ 90% of true 1RM):

| Week | Set 1 | Set 2 | Set 3 (AMRAP) |
|------|-------|-------|---------------|
| 1 (5s) | 65% ×5 | 75% ×5 | 85% ×5+ |
| 2 (3s) | 70% ×3 | 80% ×3 | 90% ×3+ |
| 3 (5/3/1) | 75% ×5 | 85% ×3 | 95% ×1+ |
| 4 (deload) | 40% ×5 | 50% ×5 | 60% ×5 |

Main lifts: Squat (S00) · Deadlift (H00.0) · Bench Press (P01.00) · Military Press (P10.0)

---

## Configurations used

### Standard 4-day split (2018-07 to 2019-01)

| Day | Main lift | Accessories |
|-----|-----------|-------------|
| SQ day | Squat | Zercher GM, Australian Pull-ups, Snatch Grip DL |
| BP day | Bench Press | Kroc Row, Chin-ups, Reverse Press |
| DL day | Deadlift | Front Squat, Ring Dips, Ab Wheel |
| MP day | Military Press | Chin-ups, Close Grip BP, Bar Hang |

Session names: `SQ - 5/3/1`, `BP - 5/3/1`, `DL - 5/3/1`, `MP - 5/3/1`

### BBB (Boring But Big) variant

5×10 supplemental at 50–60% after main work.  
Session names: `BP - 5/3/1 BBB`, `DL - 5/3/1 BBB`

### Road to PRs (2019-04 to 2019-05)

Focused blocks pushing specific lifts:
- `BP road to 135` — 4 sessions, 105 sets (BP + Dragon Flag + Chin-ups)
- `DL road to 190` — 4 sessions, 106 sets (DL + Hanging Knee Raise)
- `SQ road to 155` — 4 sessions, 138 sets
- `MP road to 82` — 4 sessions, 137 sets

### Lift-specific days (2019-05 to 2019-06)

Session names: `531 SQUAT`, `531 BENCH`, `531 DEADLIFT`, `531 PRESS`  
Each with dedicated accessories.

### Full Body variant (2019-03, 2020-05)

3-day full body:
- `531 Full Body (1)`: SQ + Dips + Inverted Row
- `531 Full Body (2)`: BP + High Bar SQ + Chin-ups
- `531 Full Body (3)`: MP + FS + PC + DL

Also: `531 FB1/FB2/FB3` (2020-05)

### A/B/C split (2019-02 to 2019-03)

- `531 {A}`: SQ + BP + Rows
- `531 {B}`: C&J + FS
- `531 {C}`: DL + MP + Chin-ups

### Jack Shit / FSL variants (2020-07 to 2021-03)

Minimal sessions — main lift only, no accessories:
- `BP 531 jack shit`, `DL 531 jack shit`, `MP 531 jack shit`, `SQ 531 jack shit`
- `BP - 531 FSL`, `DL - 531 FSL`, `SQ - 531 FSL`, `MP - 531 FSL`

### nSuns variant (2021-02)

High-volume LP derivative:
- `BP - nSuns`, `DL - nSuns`, `MP - nSuns`, `SQ - nSuns`

### 863 BBB (2020-03)

Modified rep scheme (8/6/3 instead of 5/3/1):
- `BP 863 BBB`, `DL 863 BBB`, `MP 863 BBB`, `SQ 863`, `SQ 863 BBB`

---

## Progression

After each 4-week cycle:
- Upper body lifts (BP, MP): TM += 2.5 kg
- Lower body lifts (SQ, DL): TM += 5 kg

Reset TM when AMRAP set drops below target reps.
